# Vision

## Problem
Faculty need a simple way to publish alumni profiles, volunteer opportunities, and first-job
listings for students without editing code. Students and families need a fast, mobile-friendly
way to browse them.

## Users
- **Students / families** — read-only, no login required.
- **Faculty / staff admins** — log in to add, edit, and remove listings.
- **Super admin** — everything an admin can do, plus managing other admin accounts.

## Non-goals
- Not a full applicant tracking system (no in-app applications/resumes).
- Not a social network (no comments, messaging, or public accounts).

## Success criteria
- A non-technical faculty member can publish a new job listing in under two minutes.
- Public pages load fast and work well on a phone.
